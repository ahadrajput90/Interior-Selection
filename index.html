About Us

Your home, designed around your life.

“We don't believe in one-size-fits-all. Every space we touch starts with a conversation.”

Interior Selection is a Karachi-based interior and home-improvement studio on Main University Road. We help families and businesses turn ordinary rooms into warm, functional spaces — guiding you on layout, materials and design so every rupee is well spent.

Whether you can picture exactly what you want or you're still figuring it out, we're patient, honest and hands-on from the first visit to the final finish.
